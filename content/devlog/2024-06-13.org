* Realization
When I began trying to replace my Transaction model with the Hordak Transaction and my TransactionDetail and TransactionItem objects, I realized that recording the sale meant not just creating a Transaction, but also applying the transaction to an Account. That means that my code must assume the presence of a certain Account to record the Transaction to.

I'm beginning to see that I need to create a Chart of Accounts, and I need a way to configure which account the "register" will record Transactions to.

* Default Chart of Accounts
A Chart of Accounts (勘定科目表 かんじょうかもくひょう in Japanese) is a set of accounts. There essentially five differnt kinds of accounts:

1. Asset
2. Liability
3. Income
4. Expense
5. Equity

   Asset, Liability, and Equity accounts are collectively called "balance sheet" accounts, whereas Income and Expense accounts make up the income statement accounts.

   A COA should probably have at least one of each type of account. However, each account type will have several sub-accounts.

** Example Chart of Accounts
- Assets:
  - 100 - Cash
  - 110 - Accounts Receivable
  - 120 - Inventory
  - 130 - Prepaid Expenses

** Basic Default Chart of Accounts
The above example is a "complete" example provided by AI.

The Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) can be thought of as an expense account, but for some businesses it is popular to separate it out from the rest of expenses to make calculations easier.

What, then, would a more conservative default Chart of Accounts look like?
