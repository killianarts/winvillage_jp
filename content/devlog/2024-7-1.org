* Aging report
A month ago, I had an Accounts Payable Ledger and Company-wise Procurement Ledger up and running, but when I started on the Aging Report, I realized that I was missing a key component: accounts. When I dug deeper on the problem, I realized that I needed to learn more about accounting, specifically the double-entry bookkeeping method of accounting. I took some time to learn the math involved, and then got to work on integrating the Hordak library into this project.
